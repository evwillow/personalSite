Incurra is Evan's current work: an AI product for commercial-auto claims reconciliation, built with Python, DuckDB, Parquet and the Anthropic API. It reads adjuster notes on open claims and flags the ones where a documented fact is not reflected in the carried reserve. It is a reconciliation tool rather than a prediction machine. The repository is private; the live site is incurra.ai.
