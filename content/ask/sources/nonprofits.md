Evan was the sole developer for two local environmental nonprofits in Ohio, North Central Ohio Pollinator Pathway and Sustainable Delaware Ohio. He decided the technical questions with owners who were not technical and delivered each site end to end: design, build, hosting and content. He handed both sites off, supported the owners for a few months afterwards, and both have run on their own since. The Pollinator Pathways site is a React application with keyword search and location and type filters for member groups, an interactive image gallery and a responsive Tailwind layout (github.com/evwillow/pollinatorPathwaysWebsite).
