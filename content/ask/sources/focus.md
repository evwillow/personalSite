Evan describes his work as deploying LLM features and automation into live operations for owners who are not engineers, and building the guardrails that keep them safe. He is preparing for forward deployed engineer (FDE) roles. He wrote a post, "What FDE interviews test: notes from preparing" (/posts/fde-field-guide), that collects public descriptions of FDE interview loops: the stages, the skills they keep testing, how he practices each one, and where candidates fail. The post says it is not insider knowledge and that every company claim links to its source.

Planned case studies on the homepage, not yet published: safety rails on a live trading robot (order gate, kill switch, loss rails, broker reconciliation, and the defects that got past them); jobwatch, a job-search pipeline that sweeps applicant-tracking boards, dedupes postings and tracks applications in one database; and a pre-registration instrument that locks the hypothesis, gates and analysis before a model run and refuses to run a spec edited after the lock.
